# Sable — Consistency Census: Duplicates / Contradictions / Orphans

**Scope:** all 1,182 findings in the 45 report files in this folder, plus `Sable-SUMMARY.md`, `Sable-MASTER-INDEX.md`, `index-scratch.tsv`, and `PROGRESS.md`. Read-only pass; nothing in the reports or index was edited. Every confirmed problem below was verified by the census coordinator against both findings' full texts (quoted/paraphrased from the files), not taken from the automated pass or the adjudication workers alone.

**Method.** (1) All 45 reports were segmented into 1,182 finding bodies (exact match with the index TSV ID set, including the three variant formats: RiptOPL ROUND7 group/Title blocks, THIRDPARTY-B Title blocks, POPSLoader ROUND7 main+annex merge). (2) Candidate duplicate pairs were generated by shared `path:line` citations (same path, overlapping ranges; same basename, overlapping ranges), shared backticked symbols in titles, and title token similarity within a repo — **783 candidate pairs**. (3) Eight adjudication workers read both texts of every pair and classified SAME / SIBLING / DISTINCT (+ CONTRA flags). (4) The coordinator personally re-read and verified **every** SAME pair and **every** CONTRA flag against the source texts before confirming it; one worker SAME call was overturned on verification (see D-17). (5) Contradiction hunting additionally ran dead-symbol cross-reference, negative-claim, and numeric-claim sweeps. (6) The orphan pass scanned every ID-shaped token in every `.md` in the folder, every REV-* entry, the index §4 ledger, and the SUMMARY's totals against the TSV.

**Headline counts**

| Class | Candidates examined | Confirmed problems | Dissolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duplicates (pairs) | 783 | **16 pairs** (9 full, 7 partial) | 689 pairs DISTINCT/by-design + 78 SIBLING (legitimate) |
| Contradictions | 4 worker flags + 3 sweep-derived | **4** (1 already dispositioned in-corpus, 1 note-only — see §2) | remainder of sweeps |
| Orphans | every ID token in the folder | **0 in-corpus**; 1 forward allocation in a non-corpus file | all REV/ledger/withdrawal pointers check out |

**True duplicate rate:** 16 of 1,182 findings (1.4%) carry a mechanism already filed under another number; 9 (0.76%) are full re-filings adding no new mechanism. The duplicates cluster overwhelmingly in **Round 9** (see §1.3).

### 1.4 Pattern note (why the duplicates happened)

Eight of the nine full duplicates are **Round 9 re-filings of Round 1–5 RiptOPL findings** (344, 468, 454, 411, 455, 444, 405, 416, 501). The round-9 briefs instructed workers to "skim headings" of prior reports and cross-reference instead of duplicating; the modules pass even caught 9 duplicates among its own section candidates. But the prior mechanisms live in finding *bodies*, not headings — a heading skim cannot see that -040 already files the `strcat` sink or that -169 already lists the fs_driver guard as a location. Two of the re-filings (D-2, D-11) actively assert separateness from the earlier finding and are wrong on the earlier text. Any future round's brief should require a body-level mechanism search (the citation/symbol pairing used here) before numbering, not a heading skim.

### 1.5 SIBLING pairs — 78 (legitimate; no action)

Same defect pattern in genuinely distinct code copies (forked modules across repos, or sibling copies inside one repo), each needing its own fix — the audit's per-copy filing is correct for these. They map the copy families: the usbd `hcd.c` unchecked-alloc family (4 copies: Launchstation/BDMAssault/RiptOPL/SMS), the IsoTD wrong-LUT-slot family (4 copies), the APA journal/cache families across the Launchstation↔RiptOPL forks, the ds34 defect families (X-49's census), the SMSTCPIP fork pairs (RiptOPL↔SMS), the BDMAssault↔RiptOPL bdm pairs, and the mmceman/ps2ips/smbman fork pairs. ## 2. CONTRADICTIONS

**C-1. RIPTOPL-468 misdescribes RIPTOPL-093 (confirmed; rides on D-2).** -468 states -093 "filed the *REV_1* gate in the smap_udpbd copy as a no-op" and presents itself as "the adjacent REV_3 expression, present in both neutrino copies". -093's text files the REV_3 expression at **both** copies (`udpfs_smap/src/smap.c:516`, `smap_udpbd/src/smap.c:522`). The two statements cannot both be true; -093's text is the primary source and -468's is wrong. **Disposition: correct-one-side** (-468's parenthetical; moot if D-2's merge lands).

**C-2. SMS-045 asserts the SMS-015 sites' sizes "are bounded" (confirmed).** SMS-045's Problem: "the *sizes* are bounded (see §1.3), but a failed allocation … is a NULL dereference" — said of a location list that explicitly includes "the six SMS-015 sites at `SMS_ContainerMOV.c:495,546,576,614,651,680`". SMS-015's claim is precisely that those six sizes are file-supplied entry counts trusted for `malloc` with no bound against the containing atom. Both findings are STANDING; the boundedness assertions cannot both hold for those six sites. -045's own mechanism (unchecked allocation *results*) is distinct and unaffected. **Disposition: correct-one-side** (strike or scope -045's boundedness sentence so it does not cover the MOV sites).

**C-3. SMS-123's withdrawal rationale vs SMS-160's measured counts (confirmed).** SMS-123 withdraws a suspected `>` vs `>=` off-by-one in the AAC Huffman check on the stated ground that "a script count shows the declared sizes are maximum valid indices". SMS-160 (round 8; counts "verified by two independent script methods") measures declared = actual + 1 for codebooks 1/2/4/6/10 (declared is one **more** than the maximum valid index) and declared = actual − 1 for codebook 11. The rationale and the counts cannot both be true. Consequence: the off-by-one -123 withdrew is, on -160's numbers, live in the +1 direction even after -123's check-reorder fix — which round 8's own narrative already half-acknowledges ("any fix that moves SMS-123's check before the read would still admit 1–2 past-the-end offsets") without correcting -123's text. Which side is right is a source question for the verification phase; the textual contradiction is confirmed. **Disposition: correct-one-side** (annotate -123's parenthetical with -160's counts; re-open the withdrawn off-by-one question explicitly).

**C-4. RIPTOPL-006's ELF-size benefit vs RIPTOPL-501's measured none (confirmed; rides on D-9).** -006 promises "tens of KB of ELF size" from de-globbing mbedTLS (labelled an estimate). -501, having preprocessor-censused all 109 files under both configs, states the ELF impact as none (empty TUs pull nothing at link). An estimate and a later measurement of the same quantity disagree; the measurement supersedes, but -006 remains STANDING and unrevised. **Disposition: correct-one-side** (-006's benefit line, as part of D-9's merge).

**C-5. LAUNCHSTATION-017 vs X-17 — already dispositioned in-corpus (dissolved as an open problem).** -017's original fix pointed at file-based update code; X-17 (same round) proved that code test-only dead, and the index records the correction (§4: "X-17 further corrects -017's suggested fix") with -017's status REVISED. Recorded here as evidence the corpus's self-correction mechanism works when the two texts are read against each other — the step rounds 7–9 skipped for §1's pairs.

**C-6. Header-corpus line totals across rounds — note-only.** Round 7/8 coverage text cites header totals of 35,590–35,808 lines; round 9's headers pass measured 16,029 lines for the out-of-modules/third_party subset (260 files) and states in its own report that the earlier figures "do not reproduce at this pin under any measured extension set", declaring its measured ledger authoritative. The disagreement is therefore already flagged in-corpus by the later round; the earlier rounds' figures stand uncorrected in their files. **Disposition: note-only** (a reader of the R7/R8 files alone would not know).

**Sweeps that dissolved:** the dead-vs-live sweep (Cat-1 subject symbols cross-referenced against all bodies) produced no confirmed contradiction after adjudication — every apparent case resolved to copy-scoping (e.g. `pcdiag_cf_probe` dead in Launchstation's copy, live in RiptOPL's, LAUNCHSTATION-155/RIPTOPL-241, classified SIBLING; `mmce_sio2_rx_dma` dead in both repos' copies, consistently) or to acknowledged shims (PS2SERVERS-007/-052, checked by batch D). The numeric-claim sweep found exactly one differing-value group — `bgm.ogg` 462 KB (Launchstation) vs 324 KB (RiptOPL) — different repos, different files; dissolved. The corpus-level negative claims (R7 "no ARRAY_SIZE-family macro", R7 "no self-comparisons" — the latter already narrowed by R8 and recorded as RIPTOPL-301 REVISED; Launchstation "zero siblings of -119"; SMS "0 macro candidates"; POPSLoader "no wrong size/count macro"; X-52/X-53 census negatives) are contradicted by no later filing.
