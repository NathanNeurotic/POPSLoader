## Reasoning for non-VERIFIED verdicts

### RIPTOPL-056 — WEAKENED (duplication holds; the stated divergence does not)

Evidence trail:
- Both copies exist at the cited sizes: `modules/network/smbinit/des.c` = 564 lines, `modules/network/smbman-ra/des.c` = 568 lines. The duplication itself is real.
- The finding states the load-bearing divergence is key storage: "the smbman-ra copy keeps a static `DES_Keys[128]` buffer and const-correct signatures … the smbinit copy does neither." Re-read refutes half of that: the smbinit copy **does** keep a static key buffer — `static unsigned char DES_Keys[128];` at `modules/network/smbinit/des.c:451`, declared function-locally inside `DES_createkeys`. The smbman-ra copy's buffer is file-scope and aligned: `static unsigned char DES_Keys[128] __attribute__((aligned(16)));` at `modules/network/smbman-ra/des.c:439`. So the true divergence is buffer **scope/alignment**, not presence vs absence.
- The const half holds: smbman-ra has `DES_createkeys(const unsigned char *key)` (:445), `key7TOkey8(const …)` (:419) and `DES(const …, const …, …)`; smbinit has none of the const qualifiers (:420, :444, :508).
- The remaining real divergence is local-variable placement (`s`/`u` declared at function top in smbinit vs block-local in smbman-ra). A comment-stripped, whitespace-normalised diff computed this verification gives **24 differing lines**, not the finding's 71 (the 71 figure is not reproducible under that stripping; a raw diff is dominated by a reflowed comment block).
- **Narrower truth that survives:** two copies of one DES implementation, diverged in const-correctness, DES_Keys scope/alignment, and local placement (~24 stripped lines) — the "one copy was patched, the other was not" framing survives only for const-correctness, not for static key storage.
