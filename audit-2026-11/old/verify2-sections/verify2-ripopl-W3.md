Evidence trail: `DS3USB_init` is at `modules/ds34usb/iop/ds34usb.c:290-297` — it writes `usb_buf[0..3] = {0x42,0x0c,0x00,0x00}` and issues `UsbControlTransfer(…, 4, usb_buf, NULL, NULL)` (NULL callback, nothing waits). The finding cited this staging at :305-313, which is actually the start of `readReport`. The call site in `usb_config_set` is at :271 (not :274), followed after the LED setup by `LEDRumble(led, 0, 0, pad)` at :282 (not :283), and `LEDRumble`'s wipe `mips_memset(usb_buf, 0, sizeof(usb_buf))` is at :562 (not :560; a `PollSema` precedes it, so "first act" is also slightly loose). Every mechanistic element of the finding holds; only the standalone-side line numbers are off by ~2-15 lines.

### RIPTOPL-117 — VERIFIED-DRIFT
Evidence trail: grep for the spin pattern `while ((inl_sio2_stat6c_get() & (1 << 12)) == 0)` over `modules/mx4sio_bd/src/mx4sio.c` returns exactly nine sites: 39, 97, 254, 293, 333, 375, 417, 464, 525. The ISR registration citation (:196-206) is likewise the `mx_sio2_unlock` body; registration (`RegisterIntrHandler`/`EnableIntr`) is at :180-196 in `mx_sio2_lock`. The defect (unbounded spin in interrupt context) is fully proven; two of its citations are wrong.
