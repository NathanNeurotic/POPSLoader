# vmc-to-pmc

# [VMC] **Use your VMC saves on a PS1 retail console**

______________________________________________________________________________________________________________

*[Original french tutorial by Subaru-San]* @[metagames](http://www.metagames-eu.com/forums/les-tutoriels-playstation-2/convertir-ses-saves-ps1-pour-pops-et-de-pops-pour-mc-ps1-134666.html)

##  *Software Requirements :*

- [uLaunchELF](https://bitbucket.org/ShaolinAssassin/popstarter-documentation-stuff/wiki/related-stuff)

- [MemcardRex 1.8](https://bitbucket.org/ShaolinAssassin/popstarter-documentation-stuff/wiki/related-stuff)

______________________________________________________________________________________________________________

1. [PS2 side] Copy/paste (uLE) your VMC from POPS directory to USB, then to your PC ;

2. [PC side] Launch MemcardRex, click “Open”, then “All files”, then browse to your VMC. You will see your VMC content ;

3. [PC] Select the save you want to export, click “Export”, then choose “RAW single save (B???????????*)”, then save ;

Your save is now extracted from the VMC.

4. [PS2] Time to import it to your PS1 MC. Use uLE to copy/paste it from USB to PS1 MC.

5. Done.
