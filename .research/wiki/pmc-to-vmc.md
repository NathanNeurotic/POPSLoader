# pmc-to-vmc

# [VMC] **Use your PS1 MC saves with POPStarter**

______________________________________________________________________________________________________________

*[Original french tutorial by Subaru-San – translation by ShaolinAssassin]* [@metagames](http://www.metagames-eu.com/forums/les-tutoriels-playstation-2/convertir-ses-saves-ps1-pour-pops-et-de-pops-pour-mc-ps1-134666.html)

##  *Software Requirements :*

- [uLaunchELF](https://bitbucket.org/ShaolinAssassin/popstarter-documentation-stuff/wiki/related-stuff)

- [MemcardRex 1.8](https://bitbucket.org/ShaolinAssassin/popstarter-documentation-stuff/wiki/related-stuff)

______________________________________________________________________________________________________________

1. [PS2 side] Gather all the necessary files :

- Copy/paste – with uLE – all your PS1 saves from your PS1 MC to USB, then to your PC ;

- Copy/paste – with uLE – the VMC of your game (SLOT1.VMC, in POPS directory) to USB, then to your PC ;

2. [PC side] Launch MemcardRex, click “Open”, then “All files”, then browse to your VMC ;

3. [PC] Import PS1 saves into the VMC : select the first Free slot, then “Import Save”, then browse to your PS1 MC save, select it and you’re done. Your PS1 MC save is now in your VMC ;

4. [PC] If your PS1 game saves different kind of content (game date, settings data, replay data like in this *Gran Turismo* save), you can also import them by repeating the operation ;

5. [PC] Time to save your work.

6. [PS2] Done. Copy/paste (uLE) your VMC into your POPS directory and enjoy your old PS1 saves with POPS.
