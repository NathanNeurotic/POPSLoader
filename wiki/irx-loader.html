IRX Loader

POPStarter allows the user to load up to 10 modules (as IRX files) after the IOP was reset with the POPS IOPRP. Modules have to be named MODULE_0.IRX, MODULE_1.IRX... up to MODULE_9.IRX and put in the POPS folder. They will not be loaded from the VMC game folder.

MODULE_#.IRX location :

POPS/MODULE_0.IRX
POPS/MODULE_1.IRX

POPStarter is krHACKen's homebrew launcher. POPS is Sony's PS1-on-PS2 emulator and is not distributed here — it is referenced by checksum only. “TROJAN” is POPStarter's own name for a patch file, not malware.
